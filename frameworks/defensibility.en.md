# Defensibility when anyone can build

Agents lower the cost of producing software, content and routine operations for every competitor at once. Features that took a team months can be matched in weeks. An advantage that rests only on having built something is therefore short-lived. This worksheet helps a founder find what a competitor with the same tools still could not copy quickly.

## What erodes and what holds

| Erodes quickly | Holds longer | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Feature lead | Proprietary data that improves with use | A competitor can copy the feature, not the history |
| Generic content and outreach volume | Trust and reputation with a specific community | Trust is earned per relationship and lost fast |
| Low price from automation alone | Distribution: a channel, partner or audience you reach more cheaply | Everyone automates; few own a channel |
| A prompt or a workflow | Company context: documented knowledge, procedures and decisions agents can use | It takes a year of operating to write a year of lessons |
| Speed of first build | Licences, compliance and certifications | They take calendar time and cannot be bought with compute |
| | Switching costs from integration and stored history | Leaving means losing accumulated value |
| | Network effects, where each customer makes the product better for others | Rare; do not claim one without evidence |

## Six sources, with a test for each

| Source | Test question | Evidence that counts |
|---|---|---|
| Data loop | Does each customer's use make the product measurably better for them or for others? | A metric that improves with usage; data the customer has agreed you may use |
| Customer intimacy | Do we know this segment's work better than anyone selling to them? | Interview count, retained customers, vocabulary and procedures captured in the context pack |
| Distribution | Can we reach buyers at a cost others cannot match? | Acquisition cost by channel; exclusive or hard-won partnerships |
| Trust and brand | Would a buyer choose us at a higher price because of who we are? | Referrals, renewals, references, incident record |
| Regulation and compliance | Do we hold approvals that take time to get? | Licences, audits, certifications with dates |
| Operating knowledge | Could a new team with the same tools run this as well as we do? | Maintained SOPs, eval sets built from real failures, decision log |

## Reading the result

- **No source at 2 or above**: the business is currently a feature. Choose one source to build deliberately and make it this quarter's objective.
- **One source at 3**: say so plainly in the positioning statement and the pitch; invest to widen it.
- **Data loop claimed**: check consent and data protection first. Data you may not lawfully use is not an asset.
- **Everything rests on a model provider's capability**: that capability is available to every competitor. Advantage must come from what you add around it.

## The copy test

Describe your product to a capable team with the same models and budget. Ask: what would they have in three months, and what would they still lack? The second list is your defensibility. If it is empty, the work is to make it not empty, and the six sources are where to look.
