# Entrepreneur competency framework for the agentic era

When agents can execute most functional work, the founder's job shifts from doing the work to specifying it, supplying its context, and answering for its results. This framework names the competencies that shift requires, how to recognise each one, and what a founder should be able to produce as proof.

It is meant for founders assessing themselves, and for accelerators, academies and programmes designing a curriculum. Assessment is by artefact, not by quiz.

## Three pillars

| Pillar | Question it answers | Competencies |
|---|---|---|
| **Think** with AI | What is worth building, for whom, and why us? | 1, 2, 3 |
| **Build** with AI | How does the work get done by people and agents together? | 4, 5, 6 |
| **Govern** AI | How do we stay in control, within the law and within budget? | 7, 8 |

## Eight competencies

| # | Competency | The founder can | Proof (template in this repository) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Opportunity judgement | Compare opportunities on evidence and name the riskiest assumption | Scorecard, interview synthesis, experiment card |
| 2 | Market and customer insight | Size a market from sourced inputs and state why buyers choose | Market sizing, competitor matrix, ICP |
| 3 | Business design and defensibility | Design a model that earns money and explain what is hard to copy | Lean canvas, financial model, defensibility worksheet |
| 4 | Intent specification | Turn a vague aim into an end state, constraints and a success measure an agent can act on | Agent role card, SOP |
| 5 | Context engineering | Give agents the company's identity, strategy, facts and limits in a form that stays current | Seven-layer context pack that passes the linter |
| 6 | Workflow and agent design | Split a process into rule, agent and human steps and choose the simplest structure | SOP with typed steps, role cards |
| 7 | Evaluation and improvement | Measure agent quality, cost and time, and diagnose failures from traces | Eval rubric with results, failure analysis |
| 8 | Authority, risk and economics | Set what agents may do, enforce it in systems, and keep cost per task within the business model | Approval matrix, incident log, cost per task |

Two capabilities run through all eight and are not replaced by any tool: **judgement** (choosing what deserves to exist and when the output is wrong) and **trust-building** with customers, staff and investors.

## Four maturity levels

| Level | Name | What the founder does | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | AI user | Uses assistants for personal tasks: drafting, summarising, research | Faster individual output; nothing documented |
| 2 | AI-augmented | Delegates single business tasks to an agent with written instructions | A few working agents; context lives in prompts |
| 3 | Agentic | Runs connected workflows with shared context, approvals and evals | Context pack, approval matrix, eval results, logs |
| 4 | AI-native | Designs the company around supervised agents from the start; people hold judgement, relationships and accountability | Unit economics that depend on agents; governance that investors and regulators can inspect |

Higher is not always better. The right level depends on the business, its risks and its regulators. The aim is to be deliberate about the level, not to maximise it.

## Learning path

| Step | Focus | Deliverable | Skill to use |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Opportunity and customer | Scorecard with three candidates; five interviews synthesised | `opportunity-discovery` |
| 2 | Market | Bottom-up sizing with sources; competitor matrix | `market-research` |
| 3 | Model and money | Lean canvas; 36-month model with a downside case | `business-planning`, `financial-modeling` |
| 4 | Context | Seven-layer pack with a clean lint run | `founder-context` |
| 5 | First agent | One workflow: SOP, role card, approval rows, 20-case eval | `agent-operations` |
| 6 | Market entry | ICP, positioning in both languages, 90-day plan | `go-to-market` |
| 7 | Funding | Deck outline, data room status, pipeline | `fundraising` |
| 8 | Operating rhythm | Readiness table, OKRs, weekly review running | `venture-scaling` |

Steps 1 to 3 and 4 to 5 can run in parallel. Step 5 should not start before step 4.

## Programme assessment

A founder completing a programme built on this framework demonstrates, with a working prototype and the artefacts above, that they can:

1. Identify a real problem, with evidence from customers.
2. Design a solution and a business model whose numbers hold under a downside case.
3. Give an agent the business context it needs, in a pack that passes the linter.
4. Connect the agent to the tools and data of one real workflow, with least privilege.
5. Set and enforce its authority, approvals and stop switch.
6. Show measured value: an outcome metric, an eval pass rate and a cost per task.
