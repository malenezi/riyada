# Workflow 2: Plan to funding

Six to ten weeks of preparation and meetings. Ends with money committed, or with a clear reason to continue without it.

## Stages

| # | Stage | Skill | Template | Agent prepares | Founder decides | Exit when |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Model the business | `financial-modeling` | Assumptions file | 36-month model, checks, downside case | Assumptions and their sources | Model builds with no blocking warning in the base case |
| 2 | Write the plan | `business-planning` | Lean canvas, business plan | Canvas and plan consistent with the model | Emphasis for the reader | Every number matches the model |
| 3 | Decide the round | `fundraising` | none | Amount range tied to milestones; alternatives to equity | Whether and how much to raise | Round rationale on one page |
| 4 | Storyline | `fundraising` | Pitch deck outline | Governing message and eleven sentence titles | The message | Titles alone make the case |
| 5 | Data room | `fundraising` | Data room checklist | Status table, gaps, items needing a lawyer | What to share and with whom | Essentials ready before first meeting |
| 6 | Pipeline | `fundraising` | Investor pipeline | Researched, ranked list with sources and introduction paths | Who to approach, through whom | Thirty matched investors |
| 7 | Meetings | `fundraising` | Pitch deck outline | Question bank, follow-up drafts, pipeline updates | Every message sent; all negotiation | Term sheet or a documented pass from each |
| 8 | Terms | `fundraising` | none | Plain explanation of each term; dilution arithmetic | Accept, negotiate or decline, with a lawyer | Documents reviewed by counsel |
| 9 | After closing | `fundraising` | Investor update | Monthly update draft from metrics | Content and tone | First update sent within 30 days |

## Prompts to start each stage

1. "Build a financial model from these assumptions, then show a downside case with churn doubled."
2. "Write the business plan for a seed investor using the model and the context pack."
3. "How much should we raise to reach {{milestones}}, and what are the alternatives to equity?"
4. "Draft the pitch storyline: governing message and slide titles as sentences."
5. "Research seed investors who fund {{sector}} in {{region}} and rank them by fit, with sources."
6. "Explain this term sheet clause and its effect on our ownership and control."

## Human approvals

- All contact with investors comes from the founder.
- Nothing enters the data room without the founder's review; personal and contract-restricted data are removed first.
- Legal documents are reviewed by a lawyer before signature. The skills explain; they do not advise.

## Common failure

Numbers that differ between the deck, the model and the accounts. Run the consistency check at the end of stages 2, 4 and 5.
