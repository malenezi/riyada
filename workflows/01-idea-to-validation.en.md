# Workflow 1: Idea to validated opportunity

Three to four weeks. Ends with a decision to proceed, change or stop, backed by customer behaviour.

## Stages

| # | Stage | Skill | Template | Agent prepares | Founder decides | Exit when |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | List candidates | `opportunity-discovery` | Opportunity scorecard | Three to five candidates from the founder's background | Which to compare | Candidates written as customer + problem + why now |
| 2 | First scores | `opportunity-discovery` | Opportunity scorecard | Evidence-based scores, unknowns marked 1 | Leading candidate | Riskiest assumption named |
| 3 | Desk check | `market-research` | Market sizing, competitor matrix | Sourced first-pass sizing; alternatives list | Whether the market is worth interviews | Sizing has sources; no blocking regulation found |
| 4 | Interviews | `opportunity-discovery` | Interview guide | Guide, prospect list, note summaries | Who to contact; every outreach message | Five to eight interviews in one segment |
| 5 | Synthesis | `opportunity-discovery` | Interview guide, scorecard | Patterns with counts and evidence levels | What changed in the scorecard | Scorecard updated |
| 6 | Experiment | `opportunity-discovery` | Experiment card | Test design, assets, tracking | Decision rule and cost ceiling | Result recorded against the rule |
| 7 | Decide | none | Lean canvas | First canvas, tested and assumed boxes marked | Proceed, change or stop | Decision logged with its reason |

## Prompts to start each stage

1. "I have experience in {{field}} and know {{customers}} well. Suggest opportunities and score them with the scorecard."
2. "Size the market for {{opportunity}} in {{place}} bottom-up, with sources, and map the alternatives."
3. "Prepare an interview guide for {{segment}} to test whether {{assumption}}."
4. "Here are my notes from six interviews. Synthesise them and update the scorecard."
5. "Design the cheapest experiment that tests whether {{segment}} will pay, with a decision rule."

## Human approvals

- Any message to a real person is approved and sent by the founder.
- Interview notes are stored without personal details that are not needed.
- Spending on an experiment stays under the ceiling on its card.

## Common failure

Treating enthusiasm as demand. If no interviewee gave a commitment (an introduction, a pilot date, a deposit), the score for "buyer has budget and authority" stays at 2 or below.
