# Workflow 3: Launch to traction

Ninety days. Ends with a decision to scale, change or stop one channel, based on a measured funnel.

## Stages

| # | Stage | Skill | Template | Agent prepares | Founder decides | Exit when |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Define the customer | `go-to-market` | ICP profile | Profile and scoring rule from existing customers and interviews | Segment to focus on | Fit is decidable from public information |
| 2 | Position | `go-to-market` | Positioning and messaging | Statement, pillars with proof, messages in each language | Claims that may be made | Each claim has evidence on file |
| 3 | Price | `go-to-market`, `financial-modeling` | Assumptions file | Price logic checked against margin and payback | The price | Unit economics hold at this price |
| 4 | Plan | `go-to-market` | Launch plan | Channel choice, funnel definitions, weekly rhythm | Target, budget, day-90 rule | Capacity x conversion can reach the target |
| 5 | Prepare agents | `agent-operations` | Role card, approval matrix, eval rubric | Role cards for list building and drafting; eval cases | Approval levels | Eval passes at the agreed rate |
| 6 | Run weeks 3 to 10 | `go-to-market` | Launch plan | Prospect lists, drafts, CRM updates, weekly funnel report | Every first contact; one change per week | Funnel measured every week |
| 7 | Decide at day 90 | `venture-scaling` | OKRs and cadence | Readiness table against thresholds | Scale, change or stop | Decision logged; next quarter's objective set |

## Prompts to start each stage

1. "Build our ideal customer profile from these six customers and fourteen interviews."
2. "Write our positioning and core messages in Arabic and English, each natively."
3. "Plan a 90-day launch to reach {{target}} paying customers through {{channel}}."
4. "Write a role card and eval rubric for an agent that builds prospect lists against our ICP."
5. "Here is this week's funnel. What should we change next week, and only one thing?"

## Human approvals

- First contact with any prospect is approved by a human until the eval justifies otherwise.
- Testimonials, logos and results are used only with written permission.
- Marketing messages follow consent and opt-out rules for the country and channel.

## Common failure

Changing several things at once. If message, segment and channel all change in the same week, the funnel cannot say which one mattered.
