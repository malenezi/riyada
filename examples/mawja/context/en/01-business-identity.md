# Layer 1: Business identity

Mawja is a fictional company used to show a completed context pack.

- **Owner:** Noura (co-founder, product)
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-11-10

## Company

- **Name:** Mawja
- **Legal entity:** Mawja Information Technology LLC (fictional)
- **Founded:** 2026
- **Based in:** Riyadh, Saudi Arabia
- **Stage:** pre-seed
- **Team size:** 2 founders

## What we do

Mawja tells independent café owners what to reorder from each supplier every morning, so they stop running out of milk and stop throwing away pastries.

## Mission and values

- **Mission:** Give small food businesses the stock control that chains have.
- Plain numbers: we show the owner the figure and where it came from, never a score they cannot check.
- Owner's time first: anything that takes a café owner more than two minutes a day gets redesigned.
- Fix first: when an order suggestion is wrong, we correct the customer's stock the same day and investigate afterwards.

## Business model

- **Who pays:** the café owner
- **For what:** a monthly subscription per branch
- **Pricing:** SAR 450 per branch per month, billed monthly, no setup fee
- **Revenue streams:** subscription only

## Brand voice

- **Tone:** direct, warm, practical
- **We say:** "You are 6 litres short on milk for Thursday." / «ينقصك 6 لترات حليب ليوم الخميس.»
- **We never say:** "AI-powered", "revolutionary", or any promise of a specific saving in riyals
- **Languages:** Arabic leads in customer messages; English on request
