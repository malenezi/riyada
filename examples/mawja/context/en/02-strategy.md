# Layer 2: Strategy

- **Owner:** Noura (co-founder, product)
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-11-10

## Objectives this quarter

1. Reach paying customers: paying branches from 6 to 30 by 2027-01-31
2. Prove retention: branches still paying after 3 months from 5 of 6 to 90% or more by 2027-01-31
3. Cut onboarding effort: founder hours per new branch from 6 to 2 by 2027-01-31

## Priorities and non-goals

- **Do first:** retention. A new branch is never onboarded if it delays a fix for an existing one.
- **Not now:** restaurants with kitchens, chains above 10 branches, any city outside Riyadh

## Positioning

- **We are the:** daily reorder list **for** independent cafés with 1 to 5 branches **who** order from several suppliers by phone and messaging
- **Unlike:** a full restaurant management system, **we:** need no hardware and are set up in one visit
- **Our advantage is hard to copy because:** we hold item-level order history across suppliers for each neighbourhood, which improves every suggestion

## Success measures

- **North-star metric:** branches that accept at least 5 order suggestions a week
- **Guardrails:** stock-out reports per branch per week must not rise; support replies within 2 working hours
- **Measure agents by:** onboarded branches that reach the north-star threshold within 14 days, not messages sent
