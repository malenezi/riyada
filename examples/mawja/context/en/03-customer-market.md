# Layer 3: Customers and market

- **Owner:** Faisal (co-founder, sales)
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-11-10

## Ideal customer

- **Segment:** independent specialty cafés in Riyadh with 1 to 5 branches
- **Qualifies when:** orders from 4 or more suppliers and the owner or a branch manager places orders personally
- **Disqualified when:** a franchise head office places the orders, or the café already runs a stock module in its point-of-sale system

## People in the purchase

| Role | Cares about | Objects with | Decides, influences or uses |
|---|---|---|---|
| Owner | Waste and stock-outs | "My manager already knows what to order" | Decides |
| Branch manager | Fewer calls to suppliers | "One more app to check" | Uses |

## Pains and jobs

1. "We ran out of oat milk on Friday again": lost sales on the busiest day
2. "I throw away pastries every night": owners in our 14 interviews estimated daily waste, but none measured it
3. "Ordering takes me an hour each morning": owner time

## How they buy

- **Trigger:** opening a second branch, or a manager leaving
- **Cycle:** one visit and a two-week trial; the owner decides alone
- **Budget:** paid from the branch's operating budget; owners compared the price to one part-time shift

## Alternatives

| Alternative | Where it wins | Where we win | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Memory and a notebook | Free, familiar | Works when the manager is absent | 11 of 14 interviews |
| Stock module in a point-of-sale system | Already integrated | No recipe setup needed | 3 trial customers switched off the module |

## Words customers use

Customers say "الطلبية" (the order) and "النواقص" (what is missing), not "inventory" or "replenishment". In English they say "the order list".
