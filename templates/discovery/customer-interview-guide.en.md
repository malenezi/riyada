# Customer interview guide

Thirty minutes. Learn what people already do about the problem. Do not pitch; a compliment about your idea is not evidence.

## Before the call

- Target: {{5 to 8}} interviews in one segment before changing the questions.
- Ask permission to take notes or record. Store notes in {{location}}; do not keep personal details you do not need.

## Opening

"Thanks for the time. I am trying to understand how {{role}} handle {{activity}}. I am not selling anything today, and there are no wrong answers."

## Questions

Ask about the past and about specifics. Avoid "would you".

1. Tell me about the last time you {{did the activity or hit the problem}}. What happened?
2. What did you do about it? What did that cost in time or money?
3. What have you tried to fix it? What did you pay? Why did you stop or keep going?
4. Who else is involved when this happens? Who decides what gets bought?
5. If this were solved, what would change for you? How would you know?
6. What did I not ask that I should have?

## Closing

- Ask for a commitment that costs them something: an introduction, a follow-up with the decision maker, a pilot date, a deposit.
- "Who else should I talk to?"
