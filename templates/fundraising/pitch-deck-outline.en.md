## Appendix slides to prepare

- Financial summary: three years, key assumptions.
- Cohort retention, pipeline, pricing detail.
- How agents are used and governed in the product and in operations.
- Cap table summary and previous funding.

## Checks before sending

- Every number matches the financial model and the data room.
- Every market figure has a source and date on the slide.
- No claim about customers, partners or regulators that they have not confirmed in writing.
- The deck is readable without a presenter: send version and live version may differ.
