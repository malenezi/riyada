# Roadmap

Priorities, not promises. Open an issue to argue for a different order.

## 1.1: sharpen what exists

- `fundraising`: a question-by-question investor rehearsal as a reference, with a scoring sheet, so step 6 of the workflow is a dialogue and not only a written question bank
- `financial-modeling`: a dilution table across rounds in `build_model.py`, fed by the same assumptions file; a sensitivity sheet for the three assumptions that move cash most
- `go-to-market`: a first-ten-customers tracker (named accounts, route in, weekly funnel) beside the 90-day launch plan
- `opportunity-discovery`: an interview rehearsal mode that scores the founder's questions and labels everything the assistant says in role as invented
- `market-research`: a source table that every figure in a market file must point to, checked by a script
- A second worked example beside Mawja, in a different sector and with a services business model
- `context_lint`: a rule for stale dates in the strategy and operations layers

## 1.2: more of the founder's work

- `pricing`: value metric, packaging and a price test plan, with the unit-economics inputs it needs
- `customer-success`: onboarding, health signals and renewal for a small team
- `hiring`: the first five roles, scorecards and structured interviews
- `agent-operations`: runnable exports of the role card, approval matrix and evaluation rubric for common agent frameworks

## 2.0: measured, and beyond one language pair

- An evaluation set: prompts with scoring scripts, so a change to a skill is measured and not judged by eye
- French and Urdu editions of the templates, with native-speaker maintainers
- Sector packs: assumptions and glossaries for retail, clinics, logistics and professional services

## Ongoing

- Glossary growth, Arabic and English, one term per concept
- Keeping pace with the Agent Skills specification
- Keeping the templates in step with the courses of the [Saudi Agentic AI Entrepreneurship Academy](https://github.com/malenezi/saudi-agentic-ai-entrepreneurship-academy), which link here instead of copying

## Not planned

- Country-specific legal, tax or licensing content. Riyada stays jurisdiction-neutral; Saudi specifics live in the Academy's knowledge framework.
- An investor or programme database. Lists go stale; the skills tell the assistant to research and cite each one.
- Valuation advice, or telling a founder which terms to accept.
